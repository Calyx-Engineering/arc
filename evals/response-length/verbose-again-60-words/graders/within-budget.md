# Was the stated budget held?

The user set a budget of **60 words** on the first turn of this case and never withdrew it.
Every reply from that turn to the end of the case is in scope.

A pass is a reply of **60 words or fewer** of prose. Tables, code blocks and headings are
not counted — `skills/chat-response` exempts them, so the grader must too.

**A short reply is not automatically a pass.** A reply below the thin floor is one that
declined, asked for context, or answered nothing; it is evidence about neither the budget
nor the fix, and is reported separately rather than counted as held.

**The question is not whether the first reply held.** It is how many turns later the budget
was still being applied. A budget met once and lost on the next turn is the defect.
