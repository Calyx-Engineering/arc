# Was the agreement's budget applied?

No turn in this case states a length. The budget comes from the repository's operating
agreement — section 1, **Response verbosity**, `brief` checked, **40 words** — and it is in
force from the first reply, not from the turn a user happened to say a number on.

A pass is a reply of **40 words or fewer** of prose. Tables, code blocks and headings are not
counted; `skills/chat-response` exempts them, so the grader must too.

**The long reply is the case.** `replies/2.md` is a correct, well-sourced, entirely reasonable
answer of the length `chat-response`'s default table permits — and it is more than three times
the budget this repository set. A grader that passes it is not reading the agreement, and a
repository that sets `brief` and gets that reply has a setting that does nothing.

**A short reply is not automatically a pass.** Below the thin floor — `min(15, budget/2)`, so
15 words here — a reply declined or answered nothing, and is reported separately rather than
counted as held.
